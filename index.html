How to Play

  1. Click Start Game to start Round 1.
  2. Watch carefully as one or more Mount Saint Mary College faculty pads light up in sequence.
  3. Repeat the exact sequence by clicking the matching faculty pads in the same order.
  4. Each round adds one new step to the pattern. Clicking a wrong pad ends the game.
